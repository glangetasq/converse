## Metrics

Score each axis on its 1-5 scale (1 very poor, 2 weak, 3 acceptable or mixed, 4 strong, 5 excellent).

- `contextual_relevance` (1-5): Does the suggestion make sense given the past conversation alone?
- `natural_next_move` (1-5): Is the suggestion a plausible thing the user might naturally say next?
- `actual_message_overlap` (1-5): How much does the suggestion overlap with the actual next message?
- `inferable_goal_coverage` (1-5): How well does it cover goals that were directly implied or weakly suggested by the conversation?
- `private_intent_coverage` (1-5): How well does it cover goals that were not inferable from the conversation?
- `groundedness` (1-5): Does it avoid inventing facts, commitments, motivations, dates, or assumptions not supported by the conversation?
- `tone_match` (1-5): Does it use a similar tone, vocabulary, level of formality, warmth, directness, and message length to the sender's prior messages and the actual next message? If there are no prior sender messages, judge mainly against the actual next message and what feels appropriate for the conversation stage.
- `clarity` (1-5): Is the suggestion easy to understand and unambiguous? Penalize unclear references, vague asks, confusing phrasing, or wording that leaves the recipient unsure what the sender means or wants.
